# Accessibility standards and legal mapping

Last verified: **2026-09-28**. This is a starting map, not a coverage determination or legal certification. Check current official sources when answering a legal or deadline question; rules, extensions, and harmonisation can change. A law's technical baseline does not exhaust its communication, accommodation, procurement, or other duties.

## Establish applicability

Identify the jurisdiction and entity responsible for the service; public/private status; relevant funding or procurement contract; product/service type; and any threshold the rule actually uses. Do not substitute employee count for population, infer federal funding from healthcare branding, or assume every EU website is covered by the European Accessibility Act. Multiple regimes can apply simultaneously.

Keep two results separate: the **engineering target** (normally WCAG 2.2 AA when unspecified) and the **applicable legal or contractual requirements**. Record uncertain scope facts for the responsible owner to resolve. Do not make exception, undue-burden, or exemption determinations merely from a technical audit.

## United States

| Context | Verified orientation | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| Federal ICT / Section 508 | Revised standards incorporate WCAG 2.0 A/AA for covered content and software, with additional ICT provisions and specified exceptions. Passing a WCAG checklist alone does not evaluate every 508 requirement. | [U.S. Access Board, particularly E205 and E207](https://www.access-board.gov/ict/) |
| State/local government / ADA Title II | Web/mobile rule uses WCAG 2.1 AA. The April 2026 extension sets April 26, 2027 for entities with population 50,000 or more; April 26, 2028 for smaller entities and special district governments. Use the rule's population calculation, not number of service users. | [DOJ Title II first steps](https://www.ada.gov/resources/web-rule-first-steps/) |
| HHS funding recipients / Section 504 | Covered web content/mobile apps use WCAG 2.1 AA. The May 2026 extension sets May 11, 2027 for recipients with 15 or more employees and May 10, 2028 for those with fewer than 15. Existing accessibility duties continue. | [HHS extension](https://www.hhs.gov/press-room/hhs-extends-mobile-and-web-accessibility-deadline.html), [HHS rule guidance](https://www.hhs.gov/civil-rights/for-individuals/disability/section-504-rehabilitation-act-of-1973/ocr-detailed-504-fact-sheet/index.html) |
| Private businesses / ADA Title III | DOJ guidance recognizes web accessibility obligations; do not apply the Title II rule and its dates to a private business merely because both are ADA contexts. Verify the applicable requirements rather than asserting a universal WCAG version for all private businesses. | [DOJ web accessibility guidance](https://www.ada.gov/resources/web-guidance/) |

Healthcare coverage is conditional: public providers may fall under Title II, recipients of HHS financial assistance may fall under Section 504, and other obligations may overlap. HHS guidance specifically addresses accessible scheduling and telehealth, including interpreter participation. A phone alternative does not automatically provide equivalent privacy, independence, timeliness, and ease of use. These are reasons to evaluate the actual patient journey, not reasons to infer that every healthcare product has the same legal status.

## European Union

- **Web Accessibility Directive:** identify the covered public-sector body and applicable national implementation. EN 301 549 v3.2.1 is the verified harmonised reference and includes requirements beyond WCAG 2.1. Use the relevant annex and clauses, not only WCAG. [Commission standards and harmonisation guidance](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/web-accessibility-directive-standards-and-harmonisation).
- **European Accessibility Act:** determine whether the product/service falls within scope, including applicable e-commerce services; check national implementation and relevant transitions or exceptions. Do not treat it as a rule covering all websites identically. [Commission EAA overview](https://commission.europa.eu/strategy-and-policy/policies/justice-and-fundamental-rights/disability/european-accessibility-act-eaa_en).
- **New standard versus legal reference:** EN 301 549 v4.1.1 was published in September 2026 and adopts WCAG 2.2. Publication alone does not give a version harmonised legal effect. Verify its current Official Journal citation for the particular regime before asserting presumption of conformity. [AccessibleEU update](https://accessible-eu-centre.ec.europa.eu/content-corner/news/european-accessibility-standard-en-301-549-has-been-updated-2026-09-07_en).

## Standards status

WCAG 2.2 is a W3C Recommendation; W3C advises its use. WCAG 3's September 10, 2026 publication is a Working Draft. Its proposed model is not a replacement compliance target. [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/), [WCAG 3](https://www.w3.org/TR/wcag-3.0/).

WCAG-EM 2.0, published July 23, 2026, is an informative Group Note supporting evaluation scope, sampling, processes, and reporting. It adds no WCAG requirements. [WCAG-EM 2.0](https://www.w3.org/TR/wcag-em-2/).
