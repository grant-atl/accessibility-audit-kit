# Healthcare journeys

Use only for patient, caregiver, clinician or health-service experiences. Prioritize independent access to care, accurate communication and privacy alongside technical criteria. Use synthetic records; do not copy protected information into reports or public issue trackers.

This is the general audit's healthcare orientation. For a specialist healthcare review, also use `healthcare-accessibility` when installed; it covers care-specific testing, clinical communication, document production and vendor acceptance in greater depth. This reference remains usable on its own.

| Journey | Additional checks |
|---|---|
| Find care and schedule | Provider, date/time/time-zone and availability understandable; keyboard calendar and suitable typed-date alternative; selected/unavailable states; confirmation perceivable; vendor steps included |
| Login, intake and consent | Password manager/paste/recovery; grouped questions and errors; safe data reuse; preserve work appropriately; review/correction for consequential actions; accessible signature alternative |
| Results and instructions | Test name, value, units, reference range and status associated; abnormal meaning conveyed in text; chart/table equivalent; preserve clinical meaning and defer clinical interpretation to qualified review |
| Telehealth | Join link, permission prompts, waiting room, mute/camera states, captions, interpreter participation, chat, reconnect, end-call and follow-up; test the actual supported accommodations |
| Messaging and refills | Comprehensible recipients/actions, status without disruptive focus, accessible confirmation and error recovery; avoid sending real requests during an audit |
| Documents | Title, language, tags, reading order, headings, lists, tables, alternatives, links and forms; test the generated output as well as the template; consider equivalent HTML |
| Session expiry | Accessible warning and applicable extension/alternative, safe re-authentication and preservation of work; verify timing exceptions instead of assuming “security” exempts every timeout |

Do not solve access barriers by removing authentication, weakening authorization, persisting sensitive fields indiscriminately, or hiding meaningful information from assistive technology. Avoid gratuitous medical details in control labels/live announcements while preserving equivalent purpose and visible-label correspondence. A telephone number does not automatically provide equivalent timeliness, independence or privacy.

Healthcare legal scope depends on entity, funding, service and jurisdiction. Some document exceptions do not remove individual accommodation duties. Verify current official sources before asserting an exception or deadline. Clinical understanding and effective communication extend beyond passing a web scanner.

Sources: [HHS Section 504 guidance](https://www.hhs.gov/civil-rights/for-individuals/disability/section-504-rehabilitation-act-of-1973/ocr-detailed-504-fact-sheet/index.html), [HHS/DOJ telehealth guidance](https://www.ada.gov/resources/telehealth/), [Health Literacy Online](https://odphp.health.gov/healthliteracyonline), [WCAG2ICT](https://www.w3.org/TR/wcag2ict/).
