# Healthcare journey checks

Use the rows relevant to the requested product and roles. These are additional healthcare acceptance checks, not a replacement for the WCAG matrix. Test with synthetic records containing representative long names, missing values, multiple results, long instructions, validation failures, and role restrictions. Record the actual result; do not pre-fill these outcomes as passed.

| Journey | Exercise | Observable acceptance evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Find care and request accommodations | Find a service/provider, location, accessibility information, communication options, and the next step using keyboard/AT and at mobile sizes. | Search/filter changes, available services, and next actions are perceivable. A disability-related accommodation request is usable and does not silently deny the online service. Flag differential treatment for the responsible owner without inventing eligibility rules. |
| Patient identity and caregiver/proxy access | Enter through invitation, login, MFA, recovery, and an expired link. Switch between authorized patient/proxy contexts and try a denied context in the sandbox. | Password managers and paste/autofill work; cognitive challenges have qualifying assistance/alternatives. Role and selected patient are clear to AT. Revoked/expired access has an understandable recovery path; no cross-patient disclosure or shared-password workaround. |
| Scheduling and cancellation | Search/filter, change month/year, enter or select a date, inspect unavailable/selected slots, choose timezone/location, review and cancel/reschedule a test booking. Include no availability and an expired slot. | Full dates, availability, selection, and timezone are understandable without visual position or color. Keyboard and single-pointer alternatives work. Confirmation/error status is announced, and review makes the intended appointment identifiable. A typed-date alternative, if provided, has clear format and validation. |
| Registration and intake | Complete required/optional fields, grouped history questions, insurance/upload steps, and review. Submit bad values, return to prior steps, and resume a draft. | Labels, grouping, formats, progress, and errors are understandable. Valid information remains after correction; previously supplied data is reused/selectable unless a defined exception applies. Upload instructions and errors identify format/size restrictions accessibly. |
| Consent and signatures | Read the full consent, inspect linked material, correct choices, decline where offered, sign in a test environment, and obtain a copy. | A person can inspect and review the same terms without forced drawing, dragging, visual-only identity checks, or inaccessible embedded documents. A signature alternative preserves the required legal/identity assurance. Accessibility changes do not silently alter consent terms or decide for the patient. |
| Results, medications, and clinical instructions | Read tables, charts, trends, abnormal indicators, and instructions with AT and without color cues. Test missing/out-of-range values, footnotes, and large text. | Test/medication identity, result, unit, supplied reference range, date, status, qualifiers, and relationships remain available. Chart equivalents preserve relevant data and trends. No invented diagnosis or changed dosage/range appears during rewriting; clinical ambiguities have an owner. |
| Telehealth from invitation to follow-up | Join through links, identity and device permissions; use waiting room, mic/camera, chat, file sharing, captions, interpreter admission, disconnection/rejoin, and ending the visit. | Controls expose name and state; connection/waiting/mute changes are perceivable without disruptive repeated announcements. A required qualified interpreter/captioner can actually join and remain usable with screen sharing and layout changes. Accommodations are effective for the person; captions are not automatically equivalent to interpretation. |
| Messages, refills, and billing | Choose the correct recipient/medication/invoice, compose, attach, review, and simulate delivery/payment errors in the sandbox. | Recipient and item identification do not depend on color or proximity. Submission status, failures, retry, and duplicate prevention are understandable. Consequential actions have applicable review/correction safeguards. Do not send a live message or prescription request just to prove a button works. |
| Timeouts and private communication | Leave an intake draft idle, operate the warning with AT, extend where available, re-authenticate, and inspect restored content and notification previews. | Timing requirements and actual exceptions are documented. Work is preserved only in an appropriately protected form. Notifications avoid unnecessary clinical disclosure while deliberate record access remains equivalent; timeout policy is not weakened indiscriminately. |
| Records and generated documents | Generate representative after-visit summaries, discharge instructions, consent, results, and bills from the actual templates/export pipeline. Open them in supported readers with keyboard/AT. | Title/language metadata, tags, headings, lists, reading order, table-header relationships, alternatives, links, and form fields work. Check both HTML and downloaded output, long tables/page breaks, and missing data. Template fixes prevent repeated defects; a portal scan or PDF tag count alone proves little. |
| Clinician/staff workflow | Where in scope, review queues, locate the right record, compare results, use notifications, complete documentation, and hand work to another role in a test environment. | Focus and announcements preserve task context in dense tables and changing queues. Record identity and state remain clear with AT and zoom. User customization does not suppress essential information; audit clinical decision logic separately rather than changing it as a UI fix. |
| Vendor handoffs and procurement | Follow identity verification, scheduling, payment, telehealth, document, and messaging services from entry to return/error. Request version-specific accessibility evidence and known limitations. | Actual integrated workflows meet acceptance tests in the supported browser/AT combinations. A conformance report or VPAT is supporting evidence, not a substitute for testing. Record a remediation owner, acceptance criteria, retest responsibility, and an accessible interim path with comparable privacy and independence. |

## Health literacy and communication

Put the required action and relevant next step where users can find them, define necessary unfamiliar terms, expand abbreviations, and separate urgent instructions from background information. Preserve clinically meaningful qualifiers and source values. Plain-language and translation changes to clinical meaning need qualified review; a readability score is not proof of understanding. Test with people with disabilities and different health/digital literacy needs. [Health Literacy Online](https://odphp.health.gov/healthliteracyonline) and its [people-first design guidance](https://odphp.health.gov/healthliteracyonline/ensure-accessibility/practice-people-first-design) support this approach.

Communication accommodations are person- and situation-dependent. Verify how a patient requests them and how the service supplies them, including qualified language interpretation where needed. Do not assume a relative, text chat, or automatic captions can replace the required service. Test interpreter joining, permissions, layout, and continuity rather than merely checking for an “invite” button. [Joint HHS/DOJ telehealth guidance](https://www.hhs.gov/civil-rights/for-individuals/disability/guidance-on-nondiscrimination-in-telehealth/index.html), [HHS effective communication guidance](https://www.hhs.gov/civil-rights/for-individuals/disability/effective-communication/index.html).

## Legal scope and source checks

Source status checked **2026-09-28**; verify again for a legal or deadline question. The engineering target and legal crosswalk are different artifacts.

- **HHS Section 504:** for covered recipients of HHS financial assistance, the web/mobile baseline is WCAG 2.1 AA. The 2026 extension sets May 11, 2027 for 15 or more employees and May 10, 2028 for fewer than 15. Existing accessibility duties continue. Do not infer funding status from a provider's name. [HHS deadline extension](https://www.hhs.gov/press-room/hhs-extends-mobile-and-web-accessibility-deadline.html).
- **Broader healthcare duties:** Section 504 guidance covers discriminatory treatment, effective communication, and individualized secured-document provisions as well as web accessibility. A document exception is not blanket permission to withhold accessible information from a person who needs it. A staffed phone alternative is not automatically equivalent access. Escalate discriminatory eligibility/treatment behavior to the responsible clinical/civil-rights owner; a technical audit cannot adjudicate it. [HHS Section 504 guidance](https://www.hhs.gov/civil-rights/for-individuals/disability/section-504-rehabilitation-act-of-1973/ocr-detailed-504-fact-sheet/index.html).
- **Overlapping rules:** public providers may also fall under ADA Title II; private public accommodations can have Title III duties; other healthcare nondiscrimination laws, local rules, and contracts may apply. Identify the entity and jurisdiction before assigning obligations. For public entities, use DOJ's current population definitions and dates. [DOJ Title II guidance](https://www.ada.gov/resources/web-rule-first-steps/), [DOJ web-accessibility guidance](https://www.ada.gov/resources/web-guidance/).
- **Outside the United States:** verify the local healthcare and accessibility regime. In the EU, distinguish public-sector WAD coverage from specifically covered EAA products/services; healthcare branding alone does not establish EAA scope. [European Commission EAA overview](https://commission.europa.eu/strategy-and-policy/policies/justice-and-fundamental-rights/disability/european-accessibility-act-eaa_en).

For technical requirements use [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) and [WCAG2ICT](https://www.w3.org/TR/wcag2ict/). A full review includes complete care processes and their documents/vendors; scanner scores and this checklist do not constitute conformance or legal certification.
